What goes in the file

Always in: name, title, company, location, years of experience, skills (this is your whole tag list, including any tags you added yourself), links, summary and work history. Columns nobody has anything in are left out. Never in: colors, review marks or links to this app.

About and work history go in as written, except that email addresses, phone numbers and profile links in them are taken out unless you include email and phone. Read them in the preview before you send the file.

Your notes

Notes can hold pay talk and private remarks. They go into the file as written.

Notes go out word for word and may hold pay, other interviews or private remarks. Read them first.
