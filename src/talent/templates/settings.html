Cautious mode

Cautious mode makes your AI app ask you before it makes bigger changes. For example, it can't move someone to Hired until you press Keep on Review.

In detail, when it's on, your AI app's changes to saved details, moves into Hired or out, job and stage changes and closes, the notes, interviews and feedback it logs, interview cancels, new times and outcomes, a client's contacts, a job's hiring manager email (a new one too), marking your own to-dos done, and Mark as sent wait on Review until you keep or pass them. Filling other empty fields and adding new people still apply straight away. Each AI session can be kept or passed in one go.

Other stage moves, a move to Offer included, are not held unless you turn on Hold every stage move.
